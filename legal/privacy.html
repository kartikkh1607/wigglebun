Wigglebun Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Wigglebun ("the app") is a desk-break reminder with a virtual pet, made by Kartik Khandelwal, an independent developer in India ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. If anything is unclear, email me at [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • There is no account and no login. I don't run a server that receives your data.
  • Your breaks, your pet and your settings stay on your phone.
  • If you turn on movement detection, your activity data is used on your phone only, to reset the sit timer. It is never sent to me or anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. RevenueCat, a service I use, checks them so the app knows whether you have premium.

What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps this in its private storage on your device:

  • your pet's name, the pets you've adopted and the accessories they wear;
  • your settings: work days and hours, how often to remind you, which break types to show;
  • your break history: which break you did or skipped, and when;
  • sparks (the in-app reward points) and your streak;
  • the sit timer and reminder state;
  • a few flags, such as whether you finished the welcome steps and which tips you closed.

I can't see any of it. None of it leaves your phone, except through Android's own backup (below).

Movement detection (physical activity)

This is optional and off until you turn it on and grant Android's "Physical activity" permission. When it's on, the app uses:

  • Android's Activity Recognition (provided by Google Play services on your phone), which tells the app when the phone starts or stops walking, running or being still;
  • the phone's step counter, as a backup.

The app uses these only to notice that you got up and moved, so it can reset the sit timer. They are processed on your phone. The app keeps a short log of recent movement events and step counts on the phone, which is never backed up, never sent to me and never shared. You can turn it off at any time by removing the "Physical activity" permission in Android's settings (Apps → Wigglebun → Permissions).

The app does not use your location.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows break reminders and, during a free trial, one reminder before the trial ends. These are created on your phone.

Android backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account and move it to a new phone. The app allows this for your settings, pet, break history, sparks and streak, so a new phone keeps your progress. Movement data is excluded. Backups are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, and I can't access them. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Wigglebun is free to download, and its features need a subscription, a free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your card or payment details.

To check whether you have premium, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). For this, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID the app creates when it's installed (not your name, email or Google account);
  • the purchase details from Google Play: which plan, a purchase token, dates, price, currency and store country;
  • basic technical details: app version, Android version, device type, language and region, and your IP address as part of the connection.

I use this only to unlock premium, restore purchases and see subscription totals (for example, how many people are subscribed). It isn't used for advertising. RevenueCat processes it on my behalf under its own privacy policy and security measures.

Crash reports

The app has no crash-reporting tool of its own. If you've allowed it in your phone's settings, Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance reports with me. I use them only to fix problems.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell or rent your data.
  • I don't share it for advertising, and I don't track you across apps or websites.
  • The app doesn't access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Wigglebun → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Android backups: manage or delete them in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups).
  • RevenueCat records: email me and I'll have the records for your app ID deleted. Google Play also keeps its own record of purchases under Google's policies.

Children

Wigglebun is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to how it's used. Most of your data is only on your phone, under your control. For anything held by RevenueCat, email me and I'll handle it within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The app's data is kept in its private storage, which other apps can't read. The app only connects to Google Play and RevenueCat, over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date, and mention important changes in the app's update notes.
